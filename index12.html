Lincoln Index Field Study

Catch field mice, mark and release them, then use recapture data to estimate a hidden population.

Catch your first sample

Mouse coats vary: brown, black, white, beige, chocolate and spotted.Blue outline = caught in first capture onlyBright pink ear tag = marked

Sampling challenge

Notice whether colour or movement affects which mice you catch. In field studies, unequal catchability can bias a mark-release-recapture estimate.

Important assumptions

  • The population is closed between samples.
  • Marks remain attached and do not affect survival.
  • Marked mice mix fully back into the population.
  • Each mouse should have an equal chance of capture.
